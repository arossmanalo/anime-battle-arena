Shield of Wrath = **jagged black and crimson**: spiked shield plates with glowing red patterns and dark red cursed flames. Black is removed from these images, so nothing visible may be pure black: give the dark cursed plates a bright crimson or white rim.

**Camera views.** The game is watched from above, so effects that cover an AREA on the ground are **TOP-DOWN** (from directly above, perfectly round, centred in a square cell, so the ring matches the real radius). Barriers around a body and hits are **SIDE VIEW**. Each row says which.
