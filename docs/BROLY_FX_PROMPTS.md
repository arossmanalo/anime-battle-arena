# Broly FX: prompts for ChatGPT

**Attach Broly's sprite (one idle frame or portrait) when you paste prompt A**, and paste it once. Prompt A makes **one image with 7 rows** for his base form. Then **attach TWO images in this order for prompt B: first the base form sprite, then the Full Power sprite.** Prompt B makes **one image with 6 rows** for the Full Power Super Saiyan form. After each, ask for the **beam body strips** (prompts A2 and B2). Send the images back and say they're for Broly.

His look is **saturated emerald-green ki with brilliant yellow-green highlights, thick shockwaves and heavy dust and debris**. Base form: deep emerald and lime. Full Power: bigger, brighter, with yellow-green lightning. Black is removed from these images, so nothing visible may be pure black.

**Camera views.** The game is watched from above, so effects that cover an AREA on the ground (slam, roar, guard ring) are **TOP-DOWN**: seen from directly above, perfectly round, centred in a square cell, so the ring matches the real hit radius. Hits, the charge and the beam are **SIDE VIEW**.

## A. Base form effects (paste this)

```
Attached is a pixel-art chibi fighter (Broly from Dragon Ball Super). Create the visual effects for his moves as ONE high-quality PIXEL-ART GAME VFX sprite sheet in the same style as the attached sprite: crisp pixel edges, strong readable shapes, saturated colour, soft glow. The attached sprite is only for style, colour and scale: never draw a character, hands or body in any effect, only the effects.

RULES
- Pure solid black (#000000) background, no text, labels, borders or shadows. Thin dark separator lines between rows are fine.
- 7 rows, top to bottom. Every row is ONE horizontal strip of frames, left to right, each frame in its own SQUARE cell of the same size, with a wide clear black gap between frames: NO effect may touch or overlap the next frame, so keep every effect well inside its square with a margin. Each frame centred, same scale across the row. Use exactly the number of frames I give per row.
- Each row says TOP-DOWN or SIDE VIEW. TOP-DOWN means seen from DIRECTLY ABOVE like a map: perfectly round and symmetrical, centred in the square, no horizon, no perspective. SIDE VIEW means seen from the side.
- The background is removed by brightness, so nothing visible may be pure black. Effects fade smoothly to black at their edges. No hard dark outlines around glows.
- COLOUR: saturated emerald green with bright lime and yellow-green highlights and white-hot cores. Heavy, thick, chunky shapes (this is a brute, not a graceful fighter).

ROW 1 (SIDE VIEW): HEAVY PUNCH HIT (his basic attack). 6 frames: a thick bright green-white impact burst with a short shockwave ring and a puff of dust, bigger in frame 2, then fading into green sparks and dust. Small to medium.

ROW 2 (SIDE VIEW): GIGANTIC CHARGE. 12 frames: frames 1-5 = the start: a dust and green energy burst at the feet with thick speed streaks pointing LEFT (he charges right), growing. Frames 6-12 = the impact: a huge shoulder-crash shockwave: a bright green-white flash, a thick curved shockwave wall, flying rocks and debris, then fading.

ROW 3 (SIDE VIEW): SAIYAN IRON GUARD (a looping armour aura around a big body). 10 frames: a tall rounded shimmering barrier of dense emerald energy with thick plate-like glowing lines on its surface, a bright rim and drifting green sparks, pulsing slightly each frame so it loops. Taller than wide.

ROW 4 (SIDE VIEW): COUNTERPUNCH (a retaliation burst). 8 frames: a very bright green-white fist-shaped impact flash with a hard ring shockwave and thick sparks, then fading.

ROW 5 (TOP-DOWN): GIGANTIC SLAM. 12 frames: a ground slam seen from above: frames 1-3 = a green impact flash at the centre; frames 4-12 = thick cracks radiating outward in a star pattern from the centre, a round shockwave ring expanding to about 80% of the frame, flying debris, green energy leaking from the cracks, then fading to dust.

ROW 6 (TOP-DOWN): PRIMAL ROAR. 12 frames: frames 1-4 = green energy gathering inward to a bright core at the centre; frames 5-12 = a violent circular eruption: a thick expanding ring of emerald energy with a white-hot rim, wind lines radiating outward, debris, growing until it almost fills the square frame, then dissipating.

ROW 7 (SIDE VIEW): GIGANTIC ROAR, CHARGE-UP. 14 frames: frames 1-6 = a wide green aura erupting around the middle of the frame, a rising column of green energy with ground cracks and flying rocks (no character); frames 7-14 = a glowing green sphere of ki gathering at one spot, growing and crackling, brighter each frame, ending in a bright flash.
```
